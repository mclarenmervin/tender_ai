VERTICAL AUTOCLAVE STERILIZER     ZPL-A22 
Vertical autoclaves are also known as top loading sterilizers that come in 
cylindrical shape. The working of vertical autoclave is simple to understand; to open, 
a radial locking mechanism is fitted at top of the lid; a foot lever (pedal) is attached 
at the bottom. User has to press the pedal and lift the lid. 
Vertically designed chamber provides guaranty of safe and efficient 
sterilization even in confined spaces. 
FEATURES: 
 Vertical cylindrical top loading steam and pressure sterilizer 
 Double/Triple walled construction   
 Manual water filling & removal 
 Pressure interlock device 
 Temperature and pressure control system  
 Temperature sensor with range 100oC to 300oC 
 Temperature Accuracy ±1 °C 
 Supported on the stainless-steel legs 
 Dual jacket body with differential pressure and temperature display system 
 Equipped with leak proof joint-less neoprene/silicon/elastomeric rubber gasket 
 
SPECIFICATIONS: 
 
Working Temperature 121°C to 135°C  
Working Pressure 15 - 32 PSI 
Internal Chamber Made of heavy gauge 304/316/316L stainless Steel  
External Chamber Made of heavy gauge 304/316/316L stainless Steel 
Door  Single lid radial locking hinged type manual/automatic operation 
with safety and heater & pressure interlock device made of 
stainless-steel heavy grade 304/316/316L  grade and door sealed 
by gasket suitable to withstand temperature up to 140 °C & 
pressure up to 20-30 PSI 
All Pipeline Made of heavy gauge 304/316/316L stainless Steel 
Sterilization Period 25 to 30 Minutes 
Insulation Insulated with high density glass wool 
Timer Range 0-99 Minutes 
Safety Features Radial locking, safety valves,  pressure gauge (Analog/Digital), 
solenoid valve, over -heating prevention, vacuum breaker, low 
water level alarm & cut off device, steam release valves, 
condensate valve, mechanical timer, air valve, automatic pressure 
control switch, cut off automatically when the autoclave is dry 
Controller Digital/Microprocessor Based PID control system with 5” LCD / 
LED/ TFT Display 
Power Supply 220-440 V AC, 50-60 Hz, Single/Three phase (9KW)
