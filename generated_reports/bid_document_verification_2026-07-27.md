## Executive findings

1. The Vertical Autoclave buyer PDF states that EMD is not required, while the buyer checklist contains an EMD column and records EMD-related YES/NO results. EMD must not be used as a disqualification ground for this bid unless a valid corrigendum or separate governing ATC clearly changed the system-generated condition.
2. The GeM bid disclaimer in the supplied buyer PDFs says that mandating physical-form documents as a prerequisite to qualify bidders is impermissible. The checklist nevertheless disqualifies/penalizes bidders using "hard copy received." Legal/procurement review is required before relying on that field.
3. The wheelchair archive bidder names do not match the buyer evaluation screenshots. The archives contain APEX CORPORATION, CARE ENTERPRISE, Jinendra Healthcare, and SHIV TRADING. The screenshots contain APEX CORPORATION, BHAVNA STEEL EQUIPMENTS, SHIV TRADING, and VIROMI CARE. These identities must not be merged without a GeM participant list or documentary proof of a name/legal-entity change.
4. Several seller attachments reference older or different GeM bid numbers. A certificate is not bid-compliant merely because it is the right document type; the addressee, product, bidder/OEM, validity date, and bid reference must also match.
5. The two Autoclave-10 ZIPs are two halves of the same bidder set, not two separate bids.

## Mandatory verification protocol before signing the evaluation

For every bidder and every criterion:

1. Confirm the legal bidder name, PAN, GSTIN, and Udyam entity are the same entity.
2. Confirm the document is addressed to the correct buyer and cites the correct GeM bid number.
3. Confirm the offered product, model, OEM, and authorization chain match the GeM catalogue offer.
4. Confirm certificate validity covered the bid opening date.
5. Verify ISO scope, issuing certification body, accreditation, and certificate number online where possible.
6. Verify GST, PAN, Udyam, medical-device licence/CDSCO registration, and OEM authorization authenticity through the relevant official system or issuing authority.
7. For turnover, use certified audited statements or CA/CMA certificate for the precise required financial years and calculate the required average.
8. For experience and past performance, verify contracts plus invoice/CRAC/acceptance evidence, product similarity, government/PSU customer, financial year, and required quantity.
9. Distinguish bid eligibility from purchase preference and EMD exemption. Failure to qualify for a preference does not automatically mean technical disqualification.
10. Record the exact PDF filename and page number supporting every YES/NO decision.
11. Give clarification opportunity where the bid/GTC permits clarification; do not allow a bidder to replace a substantively missing mandatory document if that would amount to bid improvement.
12. Obtain procurement/legal approval before using hard-copy receipt as a qualification condition.
